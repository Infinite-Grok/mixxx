Hercules DJControl Inpulse T7 mapping (beta 0.9.0)

Files: Hercules_DJControl_Inpulse_T7.midi.xml,
       Hercules-DJControl-Inpulse-T7-script.js,
       Hercules-DJControl-Inpulse-T7-platter.js

Author:  Infinite-Grok (developed with AI assistance, reviewed and tested on
         hardware by the author; see the file headers).
Source:  https://github.com/Infinite-Grok/mixxx-hercules-t7 (main, commit
         1ec59320c4ccf5681d5a989d9b7d0447da48aa69), copied here unmodified.
Licence: GPL-2.0-or-later, the same as Mixxx (see COPYING at the repository
         root). The script derives its crossfader-curve and channel layout
         behaviour from the Hercules DJControl Inpulse 500 mapping by
         Ev3nt1ne, DJ Phatso and resetreboot (also in this directory).

Mixxx has no T7 mapping of its own, so these file names do not clash with an
upstream mapping. The mapping targets Mixxx 2.5.6 or later.
